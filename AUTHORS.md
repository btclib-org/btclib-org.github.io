# Authors

To see the list of authors for copyright purposes, see the revision
history in source control:
<https://github.com/btclib-org/btclib-org.github.io/graphs/contributors>

The homepage this repository serves is not among them. `index.md` is
generated from `btclib-org/.github`'s `profile/README.md`, whose authors
are that repository's own graph.

Part of the tree is somebody else's work rather than this repository's,
and is here on those terms:

- `_layouts/default.html` and `assets/css/style.scss` are
  [`pages-themes/minimal`](https://github.com/pages-themes/minimal)'s,
  under its own licence. Neither is a copy of the theme's file and
  nothing else: this tree's own parts are fenced inside the first and
  appended below the second, and
  `.github/scripts/check-theme-copies.sh` is what keeps everything in
  each of them outside those parts the theme's.
- `assets/img/dgi-logo-extended-black.svg` and
  `assets/img/checksig-logo-black.svg` are each supporter's own logo,
  byte for byte what their press kit serves. `_config.yml` records the
  addresses and the command that compares a copy with what is served
  there. They are marks belonging to their owners, carried here to
  credit them and for nothing else.

What is authored here is the site's configuration, the workflows, the
scripts, and the parts of those two theme files this tree marks as its
own.
